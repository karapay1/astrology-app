The twelve houses

Your birth chart is divided into twelve houses — each one a different zone of life. Think of them like rooms in a house: one for relationships, one for career, one for home, and so on. The planets that sit in each room bring their energy there. Empty rooms aren't empty lives — they're simply areas that unfold through other channels.
