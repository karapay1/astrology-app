## Personality — Ascendant in Virgo
*Virgo — the discerning perfectionist who refines and serves*

Your rising sign is Virgo, ruled by Mercury — which makes you analytical, precise, practical and quietly brilliant. You notice details others miss, you like things done properly, and you have a natural instinct to improve, fix and refine whatever you touch. There is a modesty to Virgo too: you would often rather be genuinely useful behind the scenes than grab the spotlight. You lead with the mind and with competence, and you hold yourself (and sometimes others) to a high standard.

Here is the standout feature of your chart: your ruling planet Mercury sits in its own sign of Gemini, right at the top of your chart in the 10th house of career. Because Mercury rules both your 1st house (you) and your 10th house (career and public life), and it is strong and dignified there, this forms a genuine raja yoga — a powerful signature linking your very identity to your work and public standing. Your intelligence, communication and versatility are meant to carry you far professionally; you are wired to build a career out of your mind.

But sitting right on your ascendant is Ketu, the south node, in Virgo — and this is the other defining thread. Ketu here gives you a spiritual, detached, questioning relationship with your own identity: you may feel a little 'different,' never quite defined by outer labels, and prone to intense self-scrutiny (Virgo perfectionism plus Ketu's dissatisfaction can be a harsh inner critic). It also gives real intuitive and analytical gifts. With Rahu opposite in the 7th, there is a lifelong dance between standing as your own person and losing (then finding) yourself in relationships.

A few things to keep in mind: your inner critic is the thing to manage — you are almost certainly harder on yourself than anyone else is, so let 'good enough' be enough more often. Lean into your Mercury-driven strengths (analysis, communication, skill), and give the Ketu-in-lagna part of you real room for solitude, spirituality and meaning, which you genuinely need to feel whole.
